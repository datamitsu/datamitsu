# Datamitsu

> Your toolchain deserves a home.

Documentation embedded in this binary, matching its exact version. No network access.

Fetch any page with: `datamitsu llms <slug>`.
Machine-readable list:  `datamitsu llms --list --json`.
Standard llms.txt form: `datamitsu llms --web`.
